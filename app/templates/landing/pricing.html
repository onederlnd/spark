Common questions
Can I switch plans later?
Yes — upgrade or downgrade any time. If you upgrade mid-cycle, you're only charged the prorated difference. If you downgrade, the change takes effect at the next billing date.
What happens to my classrooms if I downgrade?
Your data is always safe. If you drop below the free tier limits (1 classroom, 30 learners), existing classrooms become read-only until you either upgrade again or archive the extras.
Does the Organization plan cover all my teachers?
Yes. Any teacher provisioned under your organization automatically gets full Instructor-tier access — no separate subscriptions needed.
Is there a free trial?
The Instructor plan comes with a free trial so you can test the full feature set before being charged. No credit card required to start.
What payment methods do you accept?
All major credit and debit cards via Stripe. Purchase orders for school districts — contact us.
